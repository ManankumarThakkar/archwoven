- **Endpoints recall is the strong axis** — controllers/routers and their routes are mechanically enumerable and we get them, with per-endpoint evidence.
- **Edges are the weak axis.** petclinic yields 7 edges for 18 nodes: static analysis resolves `@Autowired` field wiring, but indirect dispatch (interfaces with multiple impls, reflection, event bus) is not followed. - petclinic's 62 ingest gaps are the safety filter's ledger: `node_modules`/`target`/binary skips, each entry `path: reason` — nothing dropped silently.
